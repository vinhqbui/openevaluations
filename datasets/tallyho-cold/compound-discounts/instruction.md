Stacked percentage discounts are added together, so two 50 % coupons make an order free. Make percentage discounts compound instead: each one is taken off what the previous percentage discounts left, in the order given. Fixed-amount discounts still come after all percentages and the result never goes below zero. The per-discount (code, amount off) list must keep the order the discounts were given in, and its amounts must add up exactly to the total reduction.
