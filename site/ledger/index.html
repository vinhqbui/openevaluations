The ledger

Scholion Cloud works its own backlog (the foundry): agents build, personas review, a human approves every merge. Each week, per repository: what a merged feature cost, and how many times a human stepped in per feature.

This is a ledger, not an evaluation. It records one team's real work; there is no control arm, the tasks are whatever the backlog held, and a week has 2 to 10 merged features. No claim is made from it, about Scholion or about any agent. It is not a tracker series. What is counted, how a reverted feature counts and what is left out: PREREG-ledger.md.

How to read it

  • Cost per merged feature: every priced token on the feature's task (the builder's and its reviewers'), from the daemon's priced_usage, divided by the features merged that week. Teams-path agents run on a subscription; their tokens are priced at list prices, not what was paid.
  • Interventions per feature: board decisions (approve, request changes, merge) and runner escalations on the week's merged features, divided by them. In the foundry every merge is a human's board decision, so about one per feature is expected.
  • Paths: teams is the director's agents working in Scholion task workspaces; autopilot is the Scholion Cloud runner taking small items on its own. A path is derived from the persona that owned the task.
